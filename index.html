      Welcome to Comedy Corner

      There’s nothing like a good joke to brighten up your day - and these jokes from Robin are nothing like good jokes, so only read them if you are already having a good day 😉

      How it works

      • Use Next joke to work through the jokes in a randomly shuffled order. Once you have seen every available joke, the sequence wraps around and starts again.
      • Use Previous to return to jokes you have already seen during your current visit.
      • Select Favourite to save a joke in the Favourites tab. Select it again, or use the × beside a saved joke, to remove it.
      • Select Hide this to stop a joke appearing in the main sequence. Hidden jokes can be restored at any time from the Hidden tab.
      • Use the Submit tab to send Robin one of your own favourite jokes. Submissions are reviewed before they are added to Comedy Corner.

      Saving your choices

      Your favourites and hidden jokes are stored in this browser on this device. They should still be here when you reopen the app, but they will not automatically transfer to another browser or device, and clearing browser data may remove them.

      Choices that you favourite or hide are periodically reported back to base so we can see which you like best, and which are the real dogs. This is done completely anonymously.

      Each joke has its own stable internal ID, so your saved choices are designed to remain attached to the right joke even when the collection is reordered or updated.
